\subsection*{Description}
A stack of conical shells is simulated. Hyperbolic version. To intersect the Wolter I plates we take advatage of the azimuthal symmetry and only consider the radial component of the photon's wavevector.

Example: Ring\_h( pore\_th=0, ring\_nr=3, Z0=12, yheight=0.83e-3, mirror\_reflec="mirror\_coating\_unity.txt", R\_d=0, size\_file="ref\_design\_breaks.txt")
