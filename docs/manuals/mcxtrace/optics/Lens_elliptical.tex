\section{The \texttt{Lens\_elliptical} McXtrace Component}
X-ray compound refractive lens (CRL) with an elliptic profile

\subsection*{Identification}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Author:} Jana Baltser and Erik Knudsen
  \item \textbf{Origin:} NBI
  \item \textbf{Date:} August 2010
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Description}
A simple X-ray compound refractive lens (CRL) with an elliptic profile simulates the photons' movement on passing through it. Each surface is an elliptic paraboloid x\textasciicircum{}2/r1\textasciicircum{}2 + y\textasciicircum{}2/r2\textasciicircum{}2 = (z-z0)/w limited to the aperture x\textasciicircum{}2/r1\textasciicircum{}2 + y\textasciicircum{}2/r2\textasciicircum{}2 \textless{}= 1. Attenuation coefficient mu and f1 are taken from the material datafile (e.g. Be.txt), in the same format as used by Lens\_parab and Lens\_simple. Photons that miss the aperture of any surface (or undergo total external reflection) are absorbed.

\subsection*{Input parameters}
Parameters in \textbf{boldface} are required; the others are optional.

\begin{longtable}{p{0.22\textwidth}p{0.12\textwidth}p{0.46\textwidth}p{0.14\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Name} & \textbf{Unit} & \textbf{Description} & \textbf{Default} \\
\midrule
\endhead
material\_datafile & Be.txt & File where the material parameters for the filter may be found. Format is similar to what may be found off the NIST website. & "Be.txt" \\
r1 & m & Semi-axis of the elliptic aperture along the X axis & 0.42e-3 \\
r2 & m & Semi-axis of the elliptic aperture along the Y axis & 0.8e-3 \\
w & m & Depth of one parabolic surface along the propagation axis (at the aperture edge) & 0.46e-3 \\
d & m & Distance between two surfaces of the lens along the propagation axis & 0.2e-4 \\
Transmission & 1 & Currently not used by the component & 1 \\
N & 1 & Number of single lenses in a stack & 1 \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
