\section{The \texttt{PSD\_monitor\_coh} McXtrace Component}
Position-sensitive monitor with phase integration.

\subsection*{Identification}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Author:} Erik Knudsen
  \item \textbf{Origin:} Risoe
  \item \textbf{Date:} March 13, 2010
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Description}
An (n times m) pixel PSD monitor taking phase into account. As the i:th ray hits a pixel (j,k) in the monitor the intensity in that pixel will be updated as a complex sum, i.e. \textless{}math\textgreater{}P\_i = P\_\{i-1\} + p\_i exp\{-\textbackslash{}phi\_i\}\textless{}/math\textgreater{}.

By setting ratio\textless{}1 the effective pixel area becomes a fraction of the ideal (which is to divide the xwidth and yheight intervals into nx and ny abutting subintervals). This reduces the monitor effective area by ratio\textasciicircum{}2. If the centering flag is set - the monitor will treat all rays as if they hit a pixel center. This behaves as if ratio -\textgreater{} 0, but at no cost in statistics.

Example: PSD\_monitor\_coh(xwidth=0.1, yheight=0.1, nx=90, ny=90, filename="Output.psd")

\subsection*{Input parameters}
Parameters in \textbf{boldface} are required; the others are optional.

\begin{longtable}{p{0.22\textwidth}p{0.12\textwidth}p{0.46\textwidth}p{0.14\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Name} & \textbf{Unit} & \textbf{Description} & \textbf{Default} \\
\midrule
\endhead
nx &   & Number of pixel columns. & 90 \\
ny &   & Number of pixel rows. & 90 \\
filename & m & Name of file in which to store the detector images - the suffixes .abs and .arg will be added. & 0 \\
restore\_xray &   & If set, the monitor does not influence the xray state. & 0 \\
xwidth & m & Width of detector. & 0.05 \\
yheight & m & Height of detector. & 0.05 \\
ratio &   & ratio between pixel area and effective pixel area. & 1 \\
centering &   & Treat all rays as if they hit the center of the pixel. & 1 \\
nowritefile & 1 & If set, monitor will skip writing to disk & 0 \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
