\section{The \texttt{W\_psd\_monitor} McXtrace Component}
Release: McXtrace 0.1

Position-sensitive wattage monitor.

\subsection*{Identification}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Author:} Erik Knudsen
  \item \textbf{Origin:} Risoe
  \item \textbf{Date:} June 22, 2009
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Description}
Based on neutron PSD component written by Kim Lefmann An n times m pixel PSD wattage monitor. This component may also be used as a beam detector.

Example: W\_psd\_monitor(xwidth=0.1, yheight=0.1, nx=90, ny=90, filename="Output.psd")

\subsection*{Input parameters}
Parameters in \textbf{boldface} are required; the others are optional.

\begin{longtable}{p{0.22\textwidth}p{0.12\textwidth}p{0.46\textwidth}p{0.14\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Name} & \textbf{Unit} & \textbf{Description} & \textbf{Default} \\
\midrule
\endhead
nx & 1 & Number of pixel columns & 90 \\
ny & 1 & Number of pixel rows & 90 \\
filename & str & Name of file in which to store the detector image & 0 \\
restore\_xray & 1 & If set, the monitor does not influence the xray state & 0 \\
xwidth & m & Width of detector. & 0.1 \\
yheight & m & Height of detector. & 0.1 \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
