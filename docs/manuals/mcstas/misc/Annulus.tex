\section{The \texttt{Annulus} McStas Component}
A flat annulus shape without effect on neutron, for instrument display purpose.

\subsection*{Identification}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Author:} E. Farhi
  \item \textbf{Origin:} ILL
  \item \textbf{Date:} June 23rd 2009
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Description}
An inactive geometrical shape (annulus), for drawing purposes only. Derived from the \textbf{\htmladdnormallink{Shape}{Shape.html}} component

Example: Annulus(radius=0.05, thickness=0.01)

\subsection*{Input parameters}
Parameters in \textbf{boldface} are required; the others are optional.

\begin{longtable}{p{0.22\textwidth}p{0.12\textwidth}p{0.46\textwidth}p{0.14\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Name} & \textbf{Unit} & \textbf{Description} & \textbf{Default} \\
\midrule
\endhead
radius & m & Outer radius of annulus in (x,z) plane & 0 \\
thickness & m & Inner thickness of annulus in (x,z) plane & 0 \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
