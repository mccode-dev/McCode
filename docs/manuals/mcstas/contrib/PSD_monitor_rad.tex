\section{The \texttt{PSD\_monitor\_rad} McStas Component}
Position-sensitive monitor with radially averaging.

\subsection*{Identification}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Author:} Henrich Frielinghaus
  \item \textbf{Origin:} FZ-Juelich/FRJ-2/IFF/KWS-2
  \item \textbf{Date:} Sept 2004
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Description}
Radial monitor that allows for radial averaging. Comment: The intensity is given as two files: 1) a radial sum 2) a radial average (i.e. intensity per area)

Example: PSD\_monitor\_rad(rmax=0.2, nr=100, filename="Output.psd", filename\_av="Output\_av.psd")

\subsection*{Input parameters}
Parameters in \textbf{boldface} are required; the others are optional.

\begin{longtable}{p{0.22\textwidth}p{0.12\textwidth}p{0.46\textwidth}p{0.14\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Name} & \textbf{Unit} & \textbf{Description} & \textbf{Default} \\
\midrule
\endhead
nr & 1 & Number of concentric circles & 100 \\
filename & text & Name of file in which to store the detector image & 0 \\
filename\_av & text & Name of file in which to store the averaged detector image & 0 \\
rmax & m & Outer radius of detector & 0.2 \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
