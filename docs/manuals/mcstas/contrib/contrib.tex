\chapter{Contributed components}
\label{c:contrib}
\index{Library!Components!contrib}
\index{Contrib|textbf}

The \texttt{contrib} category holds components contributed directly by
\MCS users, rather than authored and maintained by the core \MCS team.
The \MCS team provides the same technical support for these as for any
other component, but the original authors -- credited individually in each
component's source header and reproduced below -- carry primary
responsibility for the underlying physics and its validation. Contributed
components that mature and see wide use are periodically promoted into a
core category (\texttt{sources}, \texttt{optics}, \texttt{samples},
\texttt{monitors}); several components documented in earlier chapters of
this manual (e.g. several guide, chopper and polarisation components) began
their life in \texttt{contrib}. Always check with \verb+mcdoc+ rather than
an older document for a component's current category.

\section{Contributed MCViNE sample components}
\label{s:contrib-mcvine}
\index{MCViNE}

The components in this section are \MCS ports, contributed by Fahima Islam, of
sample-scattering kernels from the MCViNE neutron ray-tracing
package~\cite{mcvine}\footnote{\url{https://github.com/mcvine/mcvine}; preprint at \url{https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.02776}}.
They cover scatterers defined by an analytic expression or a tabulated grid for
the structure factor $S(Q)$ or the dynamical structure factor $S(Q,E)$,
isotropic and single-crystal dispersions with optional Gaussian or Lorentzian
broadening, scatterers with fixed energy and/or momentum transfer, one-phonon
scattering from a tabulated phonon dispersion, a resolution kernel for
direct-geometry spectrometers, and a 2D SANS map on a grid. Every kernel exists
in two forms sharing the same kernel code: as an ordinary sample component
(this section), and as a Union process with the suffix \texttt{\_process}
(next section).

\section{Contributed MCViNE kernels as Union processes}
\index{MCViNE}
\index{Union!MCViNE}

Each of the MCViNE kernels above is also available as a process for the Union
framework (chapter~\ref{c:union-comp}; see chapter~\ref{c:union} for the
concepts). They are used like the other Union process components: they are
named in the \texttt{process\_string} of a \texttt{Union\_make\_material}
component, may be combined with other processes in the same material, and the
resulting material can be assigned to any Union geometry. The parameters of
each process are listed with the component below.
