\section{The \texttt{Wavelength\_Diffuser} McStas Component}
Wavelength-diffuser: Applies Gaussian smearing in wavelength.

\subsection*{Identification}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Author:} Viktor L. Holm
  \item \textbf{Origin:} University of Copenhagen
  \item \textbf{Date:} Aug 13, 2019
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Description}
A component that applies a Gaussian smearing in wavelength to the incoming neutron. The smearing is defined by a normal-distributed uncertainty in wavelength from 0 to +/- dlambda.

Example: Wavelength\_Diffuser(dlambda = 1)

\subsection*{Input parameters}
Parameters in \textbf{boldface} are required; the others are optional.

\begin{longtable}{p{0.22\textwidth}p{0.12\textwidth}p{0.46\textwidth}p{0.14\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Name} & \textbf{Unit} & \textbf{Description} & \textbf{Default} \\
\midrule
\endhead
dlambda & \AA{} & Sigma-value for Gaussian wavelength-smearing & 1 \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
